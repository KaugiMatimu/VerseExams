The Process

Simple. Fast. Reliable.

Getting academic support has never been easier. Four steps and our team handles the rest.

01

Submit Your Details

Fill out our secure booking form with your exam or assignment details — course name, platform, deadline, and any special instructions. The more detail, the better we can match you.

Include the platform name (Honorlock, ProctorU etc.) and login window time. For assignments, attach any rubric or brief.

02

Get a Quote

Our team reviews your submission and sends a clear, competitive quote to your WhatsApp or email within 15 minutes. No hidden fees — everything is transparent upfront.

WhatsApp is the fastest channel. Have your phone handy so we can confirm details quickly.

03

Confirm Booking

Approve the quote, confirm your booking and complete a secure payment. Your dedicated expert is assigned immediately and briefed on all your requirements.

We accept PayPal, cards, bank transfer, M-Pesa, and crypto. Payment details shared after quote approval.

04

Receive Support

Sit back while your expert handles everything. We provide real-time updates throughout, deliver results before your deadline, and follow up to confirm satisfaction.

For exams, stay reachable during the session window. For classes or assignments, we send progress updates regularly.
